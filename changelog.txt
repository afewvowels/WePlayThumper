Date: 2026-11-17
  Changes:
    - Cracked ground slowdown strength is now a percentage (0-100%), defaulting to 50% (25% slower).
  Balancing:
    - Each recent thump an enemy has taken (its fatigue level) takes 10% off its cracked ground slowdown, so thumped-often enemies get faster but stay slower than normal.

Date: 2026-11-17
  Changes:
    - The cracked ground slowdown setting is now a strength from 0 to 1 (1 = 50% slower, as before), in 5% steps.
  Balancing:
    - Shove fatigue is back on: each repeat thump on the same enemy shoves it 60% as far as the last, until it goes 10 seconds without one.

Date: 2026-11-17

    - Map setting, off by default: hard mode, where cracked ground slows players too unless they wear an exoskeleton.

Version: 0.2.0
Date: 2026-11-17
  Graphics:
    - New shockwave: a front of rounded dirt bricks that start small and grow toward the edge, with dust clouds swelling behind them.
    - The wave leaves the ground cracked like a dry lakebed: tight plates around the crater opening into big jagged cracks at the edge, deep and dark in the middle and shallow at the rim. Each cracked patch is turned a different way.
    - The crater and the cracks stay together and fade out together.
  Features:
    - Map setting: scar duration (default 3 minutes, then a 30 second fade).

Version: 0.1.0
Date: 2026-11-17
  Major Features:
    - First test build: automatic shockwave equipment (2x2, one per grid).
    - Thumps when enemy units come within 10 tiles: a ring of dirt mounds rolls out 10 tiles, shoving and staggering enemies as it reaches them, with a little physical damage.
    - Shove fatigue: each repeat thump on the same enemy does less. Bigger enemies barely budge.
    - Each thump leaves a cracked crater that fades out after a while.
    - Sounds: an explosion for the thump and an earthquake rumble for the wave.
